Run a town
The region on Design's map, or the last one designed, run day to day: things happen to the plant, households call about what their own modems show, and trucks drive the streets to fix it. Two worlds run side by side on one seed, one dispatching from calls and one with the twin.

    A simulation

    Everything in HFC Twin is simulated: the cable plant, the modems and what they report, the households, their calls, the trucks, and every score. Streets and buildings can come from OpenStreetMap, but nothing else is real, and no operator's network is shown.
